# Delete your Pocket Words account

Pocket Words is made by Fabio Oliveira. You can delete your account and the
data linked to it at any time, either inside the app or by email.

## In the app

1. Open Pocket Words and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Scroll down and tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

## By email

Send an email to **fabio@jungle.ai** from the Google account you use to sign
in, with the subject "Delete my Pocket Words account". The account is deleted
within 30 days and you get a confirmation reply.

## Delete some of your data without deleting your account

Email **fabio@jungle.ai** from the Google account you use to sign in and say
what you want removed. You can ask for any of these on their own:

- your gameplay history and word progress (your account and friends stay)
- your match records and leaderboard entries
- word reports or feedback you sent
- your push notification tokens (you stop receiving notifications)

Requests are handled within 30 days and you get a confirmation reply.

## What is deleted when you delete the account

Deleting your account removes, right away:

- your profile (display name, avatar, native and learning languages, country)
- your gameplay history, scores, streaks, word progress and match records
- your friends list and pending challenges
- your push notification tokens
- any word reports and feedback you sent, as linked to your account

## What is kept

- Anonymous aggregate statistics (for example how often a word is found) that
  cannot be linked back to you.
- Nothing else. There is no additional retention period.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. See the
[Privacy Policy](/pocket-words/privacy) for the full picture of what the app
collects.
