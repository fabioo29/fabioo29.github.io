# Pocket Words Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Pocket Words ("the app") is made by Fabio Oliveira ("we"). This page explains
what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

**Account.** You sign in with Google. We receive your Google account's name,
email address and profile picture URL, and we store your name and email in
your player profile. We never see your Google password.

**Profile choices.** The language you speak, the languages you are learning,
your display name, your avatar, your country, your daily goal and your
notification preferences.

**Gameplay.** Words you find and miss, round scores, streaks, daily goals,
practice results, ranked matches, achievements and leaderboard placement. This
is what makes the game work: your weak words come back in practice, your
streak survives a new phone, and ranked matches need both players' scores.

**Friends.** Friend codes you share or scan, your friends list, and challenges
you send and receive. Your display name, avatar and scores are visible to your
friends and on leaderboards.

**Push notifications.** If you allow notifications, we store a push token for
your device so we can send streak and practice reminders. You can turn these
off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

**Reports and feedback.** Words, meanings, bugs or ideas you report, together
with your account id so we can follow up.

**Usage events.** Basic events such as "round started" or "session length",
tied to your account id, so we can see which parts of the app are used. No
advertising identifiers are attached to these events.

## What we do not collect

The app shows no advertising and sells nothing, so there is no advertising
identifier and no purchase or payment data. We do not collect your precise
location, your contacts, your photos or any audio recording.

## Permissions

**Camera.** Only used on the "Scan a friend" screen to read a friend's QR
code. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

**Microphone.** Only used in Story Time when you choose to speak instead of
type. Speech is transcribed by your device's speech recognition service (on
Android this is provided by Google) and only the resulting text is used by the
app. We do not store audio.

## Third parties

- **Supabase** hosts our database and authentication (servers in the EU).
- **Google Sign-In** provides your login.
- **Expo** delivers push notifications.
- **MyMemory** (translated.net) translates word meanings into your language.
  Only the word text is sent, never your identity.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else.

## Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account from
Profile > Delete account inside the app, which removes your profile, gameplay
history, friends and push tokens right away. Anonymous aggregate statistics
may remain. You can also email fabio@jungle.ai to request deletion.

## Children

Pocket Words is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly
collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and, for material
changes, tell you in the app.
