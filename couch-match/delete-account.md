# Delete your Couch Match data

Couch Match is made by Fabio Oliveira. The app uses an anonymous account, so
there is no email or password on file; deletion works by identifier.

## How to request it

1. Open Couch Match, open the **Seen** tab and scroll to the bottom to copy your **account ID**.
2. Email **fabio@jungle.ai** with the subject "Delete my Couch Match data"
   and paste the ID in the body.

The account and everything linked to it is deleted within 30 days and you get
a confirmation reply.

## What is deleted

- your profile (display name, what you watch, age band, language)
- every swipe, rating and want-to-watch mark
- lobbies you hosted, and your membership and votes in other lobbies

## What is kept

- Anonymous aggregate statistics (for example how many people liked a title)
  that cannot be linked back to you.
- Nothing else. There is no additional retention period.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. See the
[Privacy Policy](/couch-match/privacy) for the full picture of what the app
collects.
